# 🌟 Light Enchanted

**Cinematic light beams, robotic moving spotlights, SCP containment fans, and volumetric micro-raytraced shadows for Minecraft 1.20.1 Forge.**

---

### 🚀 Key Features

- **Robotic Moving Head Spotlight (`moving_spotlight`):** Continuous motorized sweeping oscillation (yaw/pitch) with dynamic moving ray projection and live shadows.
- **Ventilation Suite:**
  - **SCP:SL HCZ 3x3 Industrial Fan:** Dark containment gunmetal matte casing with pitched rotating blades and protective safety grates.
  - **1x1 Compact Wall Fan (`wall_fan`):** High-speed 4-blade duct fan with right-click speed switching.
- **STR 2.0 (Simple Ray Tracing):** Micro-ray discrete collision marching, 6-axis surface decal alignment, multi-bounce mirror reflection, and stained-glass spectral tinting.
- **In-Game Live Editor:** Full RGB/HEX palette, beam width & spread scaling up to 8.0 blocks, strobe alarm patterns, and redstone triggers.
- **Zero-GC High Performance:** Fast BVH-style ray bounds pruning maintaining 300+ FPS on client.
